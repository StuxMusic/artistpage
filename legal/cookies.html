Cookies Policy

Effective: September 2026

Short version

This page sets no cookies at all — not for tracking, not for advertising, not for analytics.

What it actually uses

The only thing stored in your browser is a single localStorage value, stuxmusic_theme, remembering whether you last chose light or dark mode. localStorage isn't a cookie — it isn't sent to any server with every request, and it never leaves your device.

Third-party cookies

There are no third-party scripts, ad networks, embeds, or trackers on this page, so nothing else can set a cookie here either.

Clearing it

You can clear the stored theme preference any time via your browser's site data settings for this domain.
